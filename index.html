ABOUT JMTC

Technical Expertise.
Practical Solutions.

JMTC – Jain Mining & Technical Consultant is a professional consultancy firm providing comprehensive technical, statutory, surveying and regulatory services to the mining and mineral industry.

Established in 1999, JMTC has developed extensive experience in mining project planning, geological evaluation, mineral resource estimation, mine surveys, statutory approvals, environmental consultancy and regulatory compliance.

Our services extend from initial surveys and technical planning to statutory approvals, environmental clearances, pollution control permissions, government liaisoning, tender documentation and auction consultancy.
